Hardware controls for the road ahead.

We build physical controllers that let drivers operate navigation apps without touching or looking at their phone screen.

Luke Technology LLC designs small hardware controllers built into the driving environment, so a driver can pan, zoom, and control a map application with a physical dial or button instead of reaching for the touchscreen. Our goal is simple: keep hands on the wheel and eyes on the road.

NaviMate

Our first product, NaviMate, is a compact hardware controller that connects to a phone and sends standard button input to the foreground map application. It requires no touch input and no user accounts — press a button, and the map responds. Learn more on the NaviMate page.
